Edgar Saltus frequently reused premises and plotlines. An editorial selection has been made to avoid overlap with other stories and eligible SE productions. The following stories have been excluded:

- "Enthralled" is a plot duplicate of "The Impostor."

- Elements of "A Bouquet of Illusions" were reused twice; the frame in "The Plot" and the actual plot in the novel _The Ghost Girl_.

- "The Princess of the Golden Isles" is a plot duplicate of "The Golden Chimera" and "The Collapsible Husband."

- "The Princess of the Sun" is a plot duplicate of "The Goblin Girl."

- "Alma Aldorata" was reused in "The Ghost Story" and _The Ghost Girl_.

- "The Shudder" has the same reveal as "The Grand Duke's Rubies", and elements of the story have been reused in "The Verdict" and _The Paliser Case_.

- "Once is Enough" is a reworked version of a passage from "A Transaction in Hearts".

- "The Collapsible Husband and other meddlers in the Caxton Case" is a plot duplicate of "The Princess of the Golden Isles" and "The Golden Chimera."

- "The Ghost Story" is an early version of the novel _The Ghost Girl_.
